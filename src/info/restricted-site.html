Get Clone couldn't act on this site

Firefox blocked the action. This happens on domains Firefox restricts by default. To allow it, enable Run on sites with restrictions for Get Clone in the Add-ons Manager.

  1. Open about:addons.
  2. Find Get Clone and click it.
  3. Under Details, turn on Run on sites with restrictions.
  4. Come back and try again.

Get Clone only moves data between containers, locally, on your machine. Cookies and site storage never leave your device. You can read Mozilla's explanation of restricted domains at support.mozilla.org/kb/quarantined-domains.
